# inonu-trader

A paper-trading dashboard for testing rule-based stock strategies with historical data and tracking simulated portfolio performance.

- Import historical price data and configure strategies using simple entry, exit, and position-sizing rules.
- Backtest strategies with realistic trading fees and slippage.
- Track simulated trades, portfolio value, drawdowns, and benchmark comparisons in an interactive dashboard.
- Export trade history and performance reports for reproducible strategy evaluation.
- Keep trading simulation-only, with no real-money order execution.
