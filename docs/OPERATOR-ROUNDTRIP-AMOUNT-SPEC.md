## Proposed immutable observable acceptance

A1 — Exact per-leg evidence: a round-trip candidate/plan binds chain, token direction, decimals, exact atomic inputs, quoted outputs, conservative minima, slippage, receipt times and cost evidence. Return quote input equals the conservative executable first-leg output. A mismatch, malformed binding or changed plan/candidate evidence fails closed before approval/signing/sending. Hashes bind all quantities relevant to execution; a hash alone is not evidence of their validity. Atomic calculations preserve integer precision and the explicitly specified local rounding direction; provider rounding is not presumed documented.

A2 — Conservative economics before handoff: before the first approval or swap can be handed to a signer, the conservative return minimum for that exact input, minus committed capital and all required known costs, satisfies existing economic thresholds. Quote/cost evidence missing, expired, future-dated, invalid or inadequate cannot become eligible through inference. The independent 1%/0.5% fixture must not start a trade; rejection before irreversible effects is acceptable and expected. A separate economically sufficient fixture must remain capable of a complete synthetic round trip.

A3 — Prepared transaction and partial execution: prepared legs must be checked against their exact input-bound quote and existing identity, router, spender, selector, value, minimum, slippage, simulation and freshness constraints. If first-leg preparation changes the amount required for the second leg, recompute/requote and revalidate the complete conservative case before any handoff, or reject. Later adverse changes stop the return leg without lowering floors or selling additional wallet balance. Preserve intermediate amount, transaction hashes, exposure/caps and truthful reconciliation status after a synthetic first-leg failure; no automatic retry. Receipts do not establish attributable profit or actual available token balance.

A4 — Public regression coverage: exercise injected quote client through candidate builder, deterministic gate, plan and executor, with quote outputs/minima dependent on exact input. Cover the failing linear fixture, sufficient conservative fixture, zero slippage, one-atomic-unit rounding, large BigInt values, 6/18 decimals, non-linear return route, incorrect input echo, altered binding, absent/invalid cost and stale/future quote, changed first prepared minimum and post-first-leg adverse return. Verify effects and ledger artifacts, not only helper output. No live provider, real signer, credentials or paid decision inference in these regressions.

A5 — Preserved controls: model decision validation, mock rejection, dry-run/shadow, STOP, allowlists, existing economic thresholds, gas reserves, caps, loss stops, expiry, approvals for exact amount, simulations, receipt qualification, idempotency, exclusive CLI lock and manual reconciliation remain effective. Missing old amount binding cannot silently qualify legacy/synthetic objects for live execution. Dry-run remains usable with an explicit unqualified explanation where evidence is missing. No changes to custody or real trading authorization.

A6 — Evidence and integration: obtain new public RED against the fixed baseline before implementation; satisfy affected regressions, typecheck and relevant suite, independent exact-hash requirements review, standards review and audit. Integrate only reviewed scoped files into current main preserving concurrent changes, verify the integrated behavior, and record the remaining ecosystem/shadow/localhost dependencies separately. R1 / A1,A3 — Shared slippage: preserve the current 0.5 percent as exact decimal text, shared by builder context, candidate/plan and executor/prepare. Validate 0..5 percent without widening existing caps, and validate equality with the returned prepared value. No new environment setting or implicit autoSlippage/exactOut. The quote endpoint has no slippage parameter; local minima and swap requests carry this policy.

R2 / A1,A2 — Exact atomic minima: for q atomic output and integer percent scale S=1000000, k=5000 represents current 0.5 percent, and local minimum m=floor(q*(S-k)/S). General decimal support must have a declared precision and reject unsupported precision, not silently round. This floor is local policy, not an asserted OKX rounding algorithm. Quote leg2 directly for the bound leg1 minimum, never proportionally scale a quote for another input. A prepared minimum that differs requires complete reconstruction/revalidation before the first handoff, or rejection. Do not demand ceil based on an unverified remote convention.

R3 / A2 — Complete costs: typed cost evidence identifies source, unit/scale, coverage, upper bound, observation/validity and whether already included in output. Cover both swaps, each approval actually planned, applicable trading/DEX/tax/referral charges, inference and other required execution charges. Zero needs evidence of absence or inclusion. Quote tradeFee is an estimated network fee in USD, not a trading-fee proof or ceiling. Verified network bounds use committed transaction gas limits times fee caps, including approvals and known additional chain charges, without also subtracting the same fee estimate. EIP-1559 fee caps already include priority fees. Unknown FX, additional chain charges or inference bounds remain unknown and prevent qualification; do not substitute the inference allowance for a confirmed bill. Existing gas reserves/caps remain enforced on the actual send limits.

R4 / A1,A2,A5 — Exact economics and identity: compare conservative final atomic credit at a verified lower price bound, real atomic capital at an upper bound, and cost upper bounds using exact rationals/decimal integers. Quantized credits round down and debits round up. Preserve minNetUsd, including equality; do not add an arbitrary margin. Price evidence needs source, unit, bounds and validity; inventory observation time does not prove a price as-of. Bind identity, input/output/minima, slippage, price/cost evidence and timestamps in a canonical evidence digest. Keep replay identity stable across timestamp refreshes so new temporal evidence cannot permit a duplicate execution. Validate consistency as well as hashes.

R5 / A3,A5 — Revalidation at asynchronous boundaries: validate each quote timestamp independently, including future times, with current clock after quote acquisition and model decision. Revalidate price/cost validity and complete economics after spender lookups and preparations before the first approval. Recheck relevant evidence, STOP and expiry after simulation, approval/receipt, second-leg preparation and signer asynchronous preparation, immediately before every handoff through a synchronous beforeSubmit guard. No network inside that guard, and no claim that it controls later internal SDK awaits. After leg1, adverse changes must stop leg2 without lowering floors, increasing wallet quantity or retrying; preserve hashes, exposure and reconciliation. Predicted minima are not observed available balances; unknown receipt costs remain unknown.

R6 / A3 — Prepared execution proof: validate remote chain/mode/token/input/slippage/recipient echoes and require a specific local proof that supported router calldata binds the actual input, output token, minimum and recipient. Request-derived metadata or a successful Boolean simulation alone is insufficient. Unsupported opaque routes remain explicitly unqualified; a sufficiently proven and economically adequate route must remain capable of passing. The supported ABI/proof mechanism is an outstanding source-backed decision, not invented in this proposal.

Additional A4 cases: threshold equality and one atomic unit below; independently future/stale quotes; unknown/negative/non-finite costs or missing units, omitted approval and expired FX; divergent slippage/recipient/token/chain; altered calldata and temporal hash; evidence aging after model, preparation, simulation, approval receipt and signer preparation; refreshed-evidence replay.
